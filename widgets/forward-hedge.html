Forward hedging P&L

Do nothing, lock the forward, or buy the option: one chart, every future.

A UK importer owes USD 10m in six months (home currency GBP, so S = GBP per USD). Three choices: stay unhedged and pay whatever the spot turns out to be; buy dollars forward at F (free, binding, symmetric); or buy a call on USD at strike X = F (costs a premium, caps the worst case, keeps the upside). Drag the future spot and the volatility; watch which line you'd rather be on.

Why this matters — real examples

Every treasury in every importing and exporting firm makes exactly this three-way choice, every quarter.

Your moneyMarmitegate, October 2016

After the referendum sank sterling 13%, Unilever demanded a 10% price rise and Tesco pulled Marmite from its website. Prices hadn't moved in July because importers' forwards, set months earlier, were still paying out; the standoff came exactly when those hedges rolled off. Hedging buys time, not immunity.

→ The link: slide ST down 13% and compare the forward line with the unhedged one: that gap is a supermarket price war.

Financial marketsAirlines and airframes hedge in billions

Airbus prices aircraft in dollars but pays wages in euros; its treasury runs a multi-year rolling forward book of tens of billions. Annual reports disclose the average locked rates: a real, audited version of the flat line on this chart.

→ The link: the forward line's flatness is why CFOs can promise a margin to shareholders before the year happens.

MacroOptions before referendums

Ahead of June 2016, one-month GBP option prices exploded (overnight implied vol reached about 50%). Firms that paid the premium kept their upside when Remain looked likely and were protected when Leave won: the kinked line on your chart, priced for a coin flip.

→ The link: push volatility up and watch the option premium, and the height of the kinked line, rise with the fear.

How to use it in class

Ask: "who prefers the forward, who prefers the option, and what would make you switch?" Then raise σ from 8 to 16 and watch the option camp shrink as insurance gets dear: that is vega, one lecture early.
