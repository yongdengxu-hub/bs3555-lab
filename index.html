International Finance — interactive lab

Trade the quotes, price the options, survive the margin calls. Built to go with the autumn lectures and tutorials.

These tools turn the numerical core of the module into something you can move with your hands: invert a bid–ask quote and hunt the arbitrage, watch covered interest parity pin the forward rate, replay the real September 2022 margin-call week, price options on a binomial tree and with Garman–Kohlhagen. Everything runs in your browser, no login, nothing collected. Each tool maps to one exam question type.

Every tool ends with a "Why this matters" panel: the real episode behind it, from the sterling flash crash to the first IBM–World Bank swap, tied back to the exact control you just moved.

Covered interest parity explorer

live

Move spot and the two interest rates; watch the forward follow. Misprice it and see the arbitrage cash flows leg by leg. (Lectures 2–3 · exam Q2)

Forward hedging P&L

live

An importer owes dollars in six months: compare doing nothing, the forward, and the option across every possible future spot rate. (Lectures 2 & 5)

Futures margin simulator

live

Replay the real mini-budget week (Sep 2022) from inside a futures account: marking to market, margin calls, and where you get liquidated. (Lecture 4 · exam Q3a)

Binomial option pricer

live

One tree, three roads: replication, hedging and risk-neutral probabilities, all live as you drag u, d, r, r* and X. (Lecture 6 · exam Q3b)

Option payoff builder

live

Stack calls, puts and forwards; see payoff and profit lines update live. Presets for the straddle and the KIKO trap. (Lecture 5 · exam Q4)

Garman–Kohlhagen pricer

live

The exam Q4 machine: d1, d2, N(d) table values, call and put prices, delta and the replication portfolio, live. (Lecture 7 · exam Q4)

Swap designer

live

Enter any two-firm rate table and a bank margin; design the swap, then check the landing rates and who saves what. (Lecture 8 · exam Q5)
