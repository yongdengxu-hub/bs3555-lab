International Finance — interactive lab

Trade the quotes, price the options, survive the margin calls. Built to go with the autumn lectures and tutorials.

These tools turn the numerical core of the module into something you can move with your hands: invert a bid–ask quote and hunt the arbitrage, watch covered interest parity pin the forward rate, replay the real September 2022 margin-call week, price options on a binomial tree and with Garman–Kohlhagen. Everything runs in your browser, no login, nothing collected. Each tool maps to one exam question type.

Every tool ends with a "Why this matters" panel: the real episode behind it, from the sterling flash crash to the first IBM–World Bank swap, tied back to the exact control you just moved.
